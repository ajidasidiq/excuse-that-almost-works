The one line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week — a burst pipe flooded the flat overnight, the plumber is already inside, and the sender can't leave him alone with the keys. Boring, specific, and almost believable: the emergency is real, the reason for staying home is the plumber, and the plumber is the reason the sender can't simply lock up and go.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the excuse (105 characters, 19 words, one line) to `/output/entry.txt`. `python3 main.py --check` validates it against the gate without writing anything.
